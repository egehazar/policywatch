Each card shows the BEFORE and AFTER text of one change to a company's terms of service or privacy policy (only the changed passages that mention AI or models, with a little context). Read both and answer one label:
- NEW: the AFTER text lets the company (or partners it shares data with) use its users' content or personal data to train or improve AI / machine-learning models, and the BEFORE text did not.
- WIDER: the BEFORE text already allowed that, and the AFTER text allows clearly more (more kinds of data, generative AI, new recipients, fewer limits, an opt-out removed).
- SAME: both allow it to about the same extent (rewording, moving text, small clarifications).
- NO: the AFTER text does not let the company train AI on users' data (for example: a promise not to train, a ban on users training AI on the service, AI features or third-party models mentioned without training on users' data, or training only on data that is not users' own, such as public web data).
- UNCLEAR: the passages shown are not enough to tell.
For NEW or WIDER, also give the scope of the training use:
- THIRD: users' data is shared with other companies for THEM to train AI.
- GENERAL: generative or general-purpose AI, or AI/ML models with no narrow purpose named.
- NARROW: models for one named operational purpose only (ads, recommendations, fraud, safety, support, search).
(If several apply, the first in this order.) For the other labels, scope is "-".
Then quote the one AFTER sentence that decides it (or "-" for NO with nothing to quote).
