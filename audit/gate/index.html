How to judge

Each card pairs a ToS;DR case (for example "You must create an account to use this service") with one passage from a platform's current terms or privacy policy (Open Terms Archive).

Answer Yes if this passage, on its own, states or clearly implies the case for this platform, so that a ToS;DR curator could quote it as the evidence. Answer No if it is only on the same topic, says the opposite, or would need another passage to make the case. Use Can't tell sparingly.

Judge from the passage only, not from what you know about the company. The cards are in random order and show nothing about what any model or rater said. Answers save as you click.
