How to judge

Each update is a real change a platform made to its terms or privacy policy (from the public Open Terms Archive). Under it are statements. For each statement, decide whether this update changed whether the document says it: states it outright or clearly implies it.

Changed
The whole document gives a different answer after the update.
Only the passage
The edited passage reads differently on this point, but other text in the document gives the same answer before and after.
No change
The update doesn't affect this statement.
Can't tell
You can't decide from the text.

For Changed and Only the passage, also give the answer before and after (yes, no, or not established) and one line on why. Quote the words that decide it if you can. Before choosing Changed, search the full documents for the topic: other text may already say the same thing.

Judge from the text only, not from what you know about the company. Red struck text was removed and blue underlined text was added. A few statements come back a second time later on; answer them fresh.
