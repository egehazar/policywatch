## What it means
- **The pilot replicated on a second, disjoint set of 10 documents, by the rules fixed in advance.** Kev ranks the
  cases ToS;DR already has approved points for above the rest (AUROC .719 against BM25 .560, +.159 [+.095, +.223]),
  and 69 of its 100 top-10 suggestions state a case that ToS;DR has no approved point for on that service (Claude
  rater; GPT 73; both raters 65). The rater put 95 of the 100 at YES or PARTLY (PARTLY also covers a narrower or
  broader claim, or a weak implication). The documents are new, but two services (Bluesky, Pinterest), the candidate
  cases and the rater instructions are shared with the pilot, and the document rule was written knowing its result.
- **Kev is better than the reranker on both measures, in both tests.** Measure 1 +.071 [+.025, +.117] (the pilot:
  +.084 [+.009, +.158]); precision@10 69 against 46 (+.23 [+.15, +.32], resampling documents; descriptive). GPT
  points the same way (73 against 49).
- **Kev's lead is not shown to come from its decision-model training.** On the same shortlisted passages (the
  reranker's top 3 per case), Kev and its base family Qwen3.5-4B instruct show no difference: AUROC .721 against .715
  (the generated +0.006 is the difference of the rounded means; unrounded +.005), interval [-.018, +.027];
  precision@10 73 against 70 (+.03, with a wide interval, [-.05, +.12]). This is the fourth measure on which the base
  family showed no difference from Kev (relocation parts A and B, states-the-case, and now this), always on the
  passages the comparison covered: here only the shortlist. What separates Kev and Qwen from the reranker is asking a
  language model about each passage (exploratory: CASCADE_QWEN - RERANK +.067 [+.026, +.108]), though that is
  confounded with the question wording, since the reranker scores the bare case title.
- **Kev's shown advantage is cost.** Qwen3.5-4B instruct took 6.05 s per cell here, 5.4x Kev's 1.11 s per asked
  cell. Kev puts all of a passage's questions in one request, and this test asks 45-83 questions per passage, so the
  ratio is near its ceiling; on the cascade's shortlist (about 7.4 questions per passage) Qwen's measured 3.43 h
  against Kev's estimated 1.09 h is about 3.1x (the cost benchmark: 3.4x at 8). At 6.05 s the KEV arm's 19,804 cells
  would take about 33 h (estimated, not run); Kev took 5.4 h for the 17,645 not already cached (about 5.7-6.0 h for
  all of them by the fit). The instruct model was asked one question per request, as in every earlier test; asking
  several questions in one prompt could change its cost (the caveat in `stale/RESULT_relocate2.md`).
- **The cascade lost nothing measurable in AUROC** (exploratory, not a planned reading): Kev on the reranker's top 3
  passages per case (2,043 of 19,804 cells) against Kev on every passage, +.002 [-.014, +.017] with the same
  bootstrap. Recall@20 is .472 against .480 (Spotify .542 against .625), and its 73 YES cards are not the same cards
  as the KEV arm's 69. By the benchmark fit, Kev on the shortlist alone would take about 1.1 h (275 passages at
  7.38 s plus 2,043 questions at 0.931 s; estimated, not run), so reranker plus Kev about 2.8 h against 5.4 h for Kev
  on everything. Part D of the relocation work adopted a reranker-first cascade too (top 10 passages there, top 3
  here).
- Predictions against results: every AUROC landed in its range or at its edge (KEV .719 just under .72-.80), and
  KEV - RERANK (+.071) and CASCADE_KEV - CASCADE_QWEN (+.006 / +.005) where predicted. Precision came in far above
  every predicted range (KEV .69 against .35-.50, the reranker .46 against .18-.30, the cascades .73 / .70 against
  .35-.50). P(REPLICATED) was put at 70%.

## Why precision is higher than in the pilot (exploratory, after scoring)
Kev's precision@10 was .45 on the pilot's four documents and .69 here. It is not one rater's leniency: GPT moved the
same way (.50 in the pilot, .73 here), and the two raters agree on 206 of 230 cards (kappa .792). Where the rise
sits depends on the rater:
- Claude rater: terms of service 47 of Kev's 60 cards here (.78) against 9 of 20 in the pilot; privacy policies 22
  of 40 (.55) against 9 of 20. GPT does not show that split: terms 44 of 60 (.73) against 10 of 20, privacy 29 of 40
  (.725) against 10 of 20. So both document types rose, and which rose more is not settled.
- Claude rater: LinkedIn and Instagram 10 of 10 each (GPT 9 and 7); Bluesky's terms and Spotify's privacy policy 4
  of 10 each.
- Random controls: 2 of 20 rated YES [.03, .30] against 0 of 10 [0, .28] in the pilot, too few to say the base rate
  differs. Both YES controls are draws that landed on a card an arm also picked (N067, Snapchat, a KEV card; N166,
  WhatsApp, "You must create an account to use this service", a KEV, RERANK and CASCADE_KEV card); the 18 random
  cards no arm picked were all rated NO (GPT: 17 NO, 1 PARTLY).

## Caveats
- Both raters are language models (Claude Opus, GPT); no person checked a card. "States the case" is not "a curator
  would add the point": some cases are boilerplate that curators may skip on purpose, and some are variants of a case
  already approved (above).
- **A gap in the exclusion rule, found after scoring.** `stale/match.py` skips approved points whose quote is under 3
  words, so the tests' exclusion rule (which reads its output) never saw them, and the generated line "a negative is a
  case with no approved point on the service" is untrue for 3 of the 425 negatives: Parler "The service has
  non-exclusive use of your content" (quote "non-exclusive"), WhatsApp "Archives of their agreements are provided so
  that changes can be viewed over time" and "The posting of pornographic content is prohibited"; none of the pilot's
  134. One card is affected: N073 (Parler, a RERANK and CASCADE_QWEN card, rated YES by both raters), so those arms hold
  45 and 69 YES cards for cases with no approved point, not 46 and 70; no Kev card is affected. Without those 3
  negatives (exploratory) measure 1 is KEV .719, RERANK .647, BM25 .561; KEV - RERANK +.071 [+.025, +.117], KEV - BM25
  +.158 [+.098, +.222], CASCADE_KEV - CASCADE_QWEN +.005 [-.017, +.029]: every reading unchanged. The live check found
  the Parler case; the two WhatsApp cases are not on any card.
- Live check (2026-11-16 07:31 UTC, ToS;DR's public API, one request per service; `stale/out/missing2_live_check.json`,
  replies in `stale/out/missing2_live_tosdr/`): of the 180 card cases, one has an approved point on its service today,
