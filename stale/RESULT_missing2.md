## What it means
- **The pilot replicated on 10 more documents, by the rules fixed in advance.** Kev ranks the cases ToS;DR already
  has approved points for above the rest (AUROC .719 against BM25 .560, +.159 [+.095, +.223]), and 69 of its 100
  top-10 suggestions state a case that ToS;DR has no approved point for on that service (Claude rater; GPT 73; both
  raters 65). 95 of the 100 state it at least in part.
- **Kev is better than the reranker, now on two independent sets of documents.** Measure 1 +.071 [+.025, +.117]
  (the pilot: +.084 [+.009, +.158]); precision@10 69 against 46 (+.23 [+.15, +.32], resampling documents;
  descriptive). Both raters agree on the direction (GPT: 73 against 49).
- **Kev's lead does not come from its decision-model training.** On the same shortlisted passages (the reranker's top
  3 per case), Kev and its base family Qwen3.5-4B instruct are level: AUROC .721 against .715, +.006 [-.018, +.027],
  no difference shown; precision@10 73 against 70 (+.03 [-.05, +.12]). This is the fourth measure on which the base
  family ties Kev (relocation parts A and B, states-the-case, and now this). The lead belongs to asking a language
  model about each passage, which a reranker scoring the case title against the passage does not do.
- **Kev's advantage is cost.** Qwen3.5-4B instruct took 6.05 s per cell here, 5.4x Kev's 1.11 s per cell (Kev puts
  all of a passage's questions in one request); at that rate the KEV arm's 19,804 cells would take about 33 h
  (estimated, not run) against Kev's 5.4 h. The reranker took 0.31 s per cell (1.7 h for every cell).
- **The cascade loses nothing.** Kev on the reranker's top 3 passages per case (2,043 of 19,804 cells) matches Kev
  on every passage: AUROC .721 against .719, precision@10 73 against 69. Exploratory: by the benchmark fit, Kev on
  the shortlist alone would take about 1.1 h (the 275 passages it touches at 7.38 s plus 2,043 questions at 0.931 s;
  estimated, not run), so reranker plus Kev about 2.8 h against 5.4 h for Kev on everything. Part D (the relocation
  work) adopted the same cascade.
- Predictions against results: every AUROC landed in or at the edge of its range (KEV .719 just under .72-.80), and
  KEV - RERANK (+.071) and CASCADE_KEV - CASCADE_QWEN (+.006) where predicted. Precision came in far above every
  predicted range (KEV .69 against .35-.50, the reranker .46 against .18-.30, the cascades .73 / .70 against
  .35-.50). P(REPLICATED) was put at 70%.

## Why precision is higher than in the pilot (exploratory, after scoring)
Kev's precision@10 was .45 on the pilot's four documents and .69 here. Not one rater's leniency: GPT moved the same
way (.50 in the pilot, .73 here), and the two raters agree on 206 of 230 cards (kappa .792). The difference sits in
the documents:
- Terms of service: 47 of Kev's 60 cards rated YES here (.78), against 9 of 20 on the pilot's two terms documents.
  Privacy policies: 22 of 40 here (.55), 9 of 20 in the pilot.
- LinkedIn (20 approved points on ToS;DR) and Instagram (79) gave 10 of 10 each; Bluesky's terms and Spotify's
  privacy policy 4 of 10 each.
- The 2 random controls rated YES (of 20; at most 3 allowed) are both cards an arm also picked: N067 (Snapchat, a
  KEV card) and N166 (WhatsApp, "You must create an account to use this service", a KEV, RERANK and CASCADE_KEV card).
  A random case on a random passage of these documents is stated about 1 time in 10, against 0 of 10 in the pilot.

## Caveats
- Both raters are language models (Claude Opus, GPT); no person checked a card. "States the case" is not "a curator
  would add the point": some cases are boilerplate that curators may skip on purpose, and some are variants of a case
  already approved (above).
- **A gap in the exclusion rule, found after scoring.** `stale/match.py` skips approved points whose quote is under 3
  words, so the tests' exclusion rule (which reads its output) never saw them. 3 of this test's 425 negatives have
  such a point (Parler "The service has non-exclusive use of your content", quote "non-exclusive"; WhatsApp "Archives of
  their agreements are provided so that changes can be viewed over time" and "The posting of pornographic content is
  prohibited"); none of the pilot's 134. One card is affected: N073 (Parler, a RERANK and CASCADE_QWEN card, rated YES
  by both raters), so those arms hold 45 and 69 true new suggestions, not 46 and 70; no Kev card is affected.
  Without those 3 negatives (exploratory) measure 1 is KEV .719, RERANK .647, BM25 .561; KEV - RERANK +.072 [+.025,
  +.117], KEV - BM25 +.158 [+.098, +.222], CASCADE_KEV - CASCADE_QWEN +.006 [-.017, +.029]: every reading unchanged. The live check (below) found the Parler case; the two WhatsApp cases are not on any card.

  replies in `stale/out/missing2_live_tosdr/`): of the 180 card cases, one has an approved point on its service today,
  the Parler case above (approved in 2021). The public API lists approved points only, so pending or declined points

- The rater's first and last tool calls are 7.1 minutes apart (07:22:29-07:29:36 UTC); its tool-call log
  (`stale/audit_missing2/rater_toolcalls.txt`) shows it read only its folder and wrote its answers in 8 batches through
