## What this shows
On 20 documents of 20 services outside the 20 platforms, drawn at random before any model scored them, the page's
suggestions held up:
- 139 of its 200 top-10 suggestions were rated as stating the case, against 99 for the reranker's own top 10 (+.20
  [+.14, +.27] over documents).
- The printed p(yes) works as a label. 114 of the 133 suggestions printed at 0.90 or more were rated YES (.86), against
  25 of the other 67 (.37). The label was adopted under the rule fixed in advance. It is the third reading of the
  plan, and all three came out as predicted.

The raters are AI models (two Claude Opus subagents, one per folder); no person has checked these cards.

## What it does not show
- That a curator would add these points. "States the case" is not "worth adding": some frequent cases are
  boilerplate (for example, that the policy says how data is collected). Precision is measured over the 20-71 most
  frequent candidate cases per document.
- Anything against ToS;DR's own DocBot, which also suggests points to curators. The two were not compared.
- The boot() edge case noted when the code was hashed (an empty label group) did not occur: both groups had cards.
